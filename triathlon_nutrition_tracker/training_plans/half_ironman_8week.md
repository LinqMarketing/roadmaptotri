# 8-Week Half Ironman (70.3) Training Plan
## Peak Phase for Experienced Athletes

**Race Distance:** 1.9km Swim | 90km Bike | 21.1km Run  
**Target Audience:** Athletes with established base fitness and triathlon experience  
**Prerequisites:** Able to swim 1km, bike 60 min, run 45 min continuously  
**Weekly Training:** 6 days per week | 8-10 hours total  
**Program Structure:** Intensive peak phase with race simulation

---

## Program Overview

This 8-week intensive program is designed for athletes with solid aerobic base fitness who are ready for a focused peak phase. It assumes you've completed a base-building phase and are now focused on race-specific preparation and peak performance.

**Key Principles:**
- High intensity with strategic recovery
- Race-specific workout durations
- Multiple brick workouts
- Nutrition practice during long sessions
- Progressive taper in final 2 weeks

## WEEK 1: Establish Baseline & Intensity

## Race-Day Strategy

**Pre-Race (2-3 hours before):**
- Familiar breakfast: oatmeal with banana and honey
- Hydrate with water and electrolytes
- Arrive early for parking and setup

**30 Min Before Race:**
- Light snack: banana or energy bar
- Final hydration and bathroom break

**During Swim (1.9km, ~30-40 min):**
- Focus on pacing and breathing
- Start slightly conservative
- No nutrition needed

**Bike (90km, ~3-4 hours):**
- Start nutrition at 45 min mark
- Every 45 min: 1 energy bar or 2 energy gels
- Every 30 min: 500ml sports drink or water with electrolytes
- Aim for 200-300 calories per hour
- Maintain steady effort, don't go too hard

**Run (21.1km, ~2-3 hours):**
- Start nutrition at 15 min mark
- Every 20 min: 1 energy gel or sports drink
- Walk through aid stations if needed
- Focus on hydration - drink at every station
- Pace conservatively first 5km, build in middle 10km, push final 6km

**Post-Race:**
- Within 30 min: Protein + carbs (chocolate milk, recovery drink, sandwich)
- Rehydrate with electrolytes
- Rest, celebrate, and reflect on your achievement!

## Training Zones Explained

**Easy/Conversational (60-70% max HR):** You can speak in full sentences. This is recovery pace.

**Steady (70-80% max HR):** You can speak short phrases. This is aerobic building pace.

**Race Pace (80-90% max HR):** You can speak a few words. This is your goal race effort.

**Hard (90-100% max HR):** You cannot speak. This is maximum effort intervals.

## Nutrition During Training

**Pre-Workout (30-60 min before):**
- Banana with almond butter
- Toast with jam
- Rice cakes with honey
- *Something light and easily digestible*

**During Workout (sessions >90 min):**
- Water with electrolytes
- Energy gels or sports drink
- 1 energy bar per 45 min

**Post-Workout (within 30 min):**
- Protein + carbs (chicken with rice, protein smoothie, turkey sandwich)
- Rehydrate with electrolytes
- *Critical for recovery*

## Recovery & Injury Prevention

**Sleep:** Aim for 8-9 hours per night. This is when adaptation happens.

**Stretching:** 10-15 min after each workout, especially legs and shoulders.

**Foam Rolling:** 5-10 min on rest days for muscle recovery.

**Massage:** Monthly professional massage or self-massage with foam roller.

**Listen to Your Body:** If something hurts (not just tired), take an extra rest day.

**Cross-Training:** Optional easy yoga or swimming on rest days.

## Race Week Checklist

- [ ] Lay out all gear the night before
- [ ] Check weather forecast
- [ ] Review race course map
- [ ] Plan parking and arrival time (arrive 1-2 hours early)
- [ ] Practice race-day breakfast
- [ ] Get good sleep 2-3 nights before
- [ ] Reduce training volume by 80%
- [ ] Maintain intensity but reduce duration
- [ ] Eat familiar foods only
- [ ] Hydrate consistently

## Post-Race Recovery

Congratulations on completing your half ironman! Now:
- Rest for 5-7 days with easy movement only
- Reflect on what went well and what to improve
- Consider your next goal (another 70.3, full ironman, or new distance)
- Celebrate your amazing achievement!
