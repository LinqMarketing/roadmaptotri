# 5K Running Training Plan
## 12-Week Beginner Program

**Race Distance:** 5 kilometers (3.1 miles)  
**Target Audience:** Complete beginners with no running experience  
**Prerequisites:** Basic fitness level (able to walk 30 minutes)  
**Weekly Training:** 3-4 days per week | 2-4 hours total  
**Program Structure:** 3 cycles with progressive intensity

---

## Program Overview

This 12-week beginner-friendly program takes you from zero to 5K race ready. The plan uses a run/walk approach, gradually increasing running intervals while decreasing walk breaks. By race day, you'll be able to run the full 5K distance.

**Key Principles:**
- Run/walk intervals gradually transition to continuous running
- 3-4 training days per week with rest days for recovery
- Progressive volume increases (no more than 10% per week)
- Injury prevention through proper warm-up and cool-down
- Flexibility to adjust based on how you feel

## Running Pacing Guidelines

**Easy Pace:** You should be able to hold a conversation comfortably. This is your primary training pace.

**Conversational Pace:** Similar to easy pace, used for longer runs and recovery sessions.

**Race Pace:** The pace you plan to run the 5K. You can speak in short sentences but not full conversations.

## Nutrition for Running

**Pre-Run (1-2 hours before):**
- Banana with almond butter
- Toast with jam
- Rice cakes with honey
- Small bowl of oatmeal

**During Run (sessions >45 min):**
- Water with electrolytes
- Sports drink if available

**Post-Run (within 30 min):**
- Protein + carbs (chicken with rice, protein smoothie, turkey sandwich)
- Rehydrate with water or electrolyte drink

---

## Injury Prevention

**Warm-up:** Always start with 5 minutes of easy walking before running.

**Cool-down:** Always end with 5 minutes of easy walking after running.

**Stretching:** Stretch for 10-15 minutes after each run, focusing on calves, hamstrings, quads, and hip flexors.

**Rest Days:** Take at least 2-3 complete rest days per week. Your body adapts during rest, not during training.

**Listen to Your Body:** If you experience sharp pain (not just muscle soreness), take an extra rest day or consult a healthcare provider.
