# 24-Week Sprint Triathlon Training Plan
## Beginner-Friendly Extended Preparation

**Race Distance:** 750m Swim | 20km Bike | 5km Run  
**Target Audience:** Complete beginners or those returning from injury  
**Prerequisites:** Basic fitness level (able to walk 30 min, swim a few laps)  
**Weekly Training:** 4-5 days per week | 4-6 hours total  
**Program Structure:** 6 phases with extended base building

---

## Program Overview

This 24-week beginner-friendly program provides ample time to build fitness safely. It features a longer base-building phase, gradual intensity progression, and more recovery weeks to prevent injury and burnout.

**Key Principles:**
- Gradual progression (10% volume increase per week max)
- Extended technique focus
- More recovery days built in
- Lower injury risk
- Confidence building through repetition

## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION BUILDING (Weeks 1-5)

## Training Terminology

**Test Pace (TP):** The pace you can sustain for 4-5 minutes of effort.

**Easy/Conversational Pace:** You should be able to speak in full sentences.

**Warm-up (WU):** Gradually increase heart rate and blood flow.

**Main Set (MS):** The primary focus of the workout.

**Cool-down (CD):** Gradually decrease intensity to aid recovery.

**Recovery Interval (RI):** Easy effort between hard efforts.

---

## Nutrition During Training

**Pre-Workout (30-60 min before):**
- Banana with almond butter
- Toast with jam
- Rice cakes with honey

**During Workout (sessions >90 min):**
- Water with electrolytes
- Energy gels or sports drink

**Post-Workout (within 30 min):**
- Protein + carbs (chicken with rice, protein smoothie, turkey sandwich)
- Rehydrate with electrolytes

---

## Recovery & Injury Prevention

**Sleep:** Aim for 7-9 hours per night.

**Stretching:** 10-15 min after each workout.

**Foam Rolling:** 5-10 min on rest days.

**Listen to Your Body:** If something hurts, take an extra rest day.

---

## Race Week Checklist

- [ ] Lay out all gear the night before
- [ ] Check weather forecast
- [ ] Review race course map
- [ ] Plan parking and arrival time
- [ ] Practice race-day breakfast
- [ ] Get good sleep 2-3 nights before
- [ ] Reduce training volume by 70%
- [ ] Maintain intensity but reduce duration
- [ ] Eat familiar foods only
- [ ] Hydrate consistently

---

**Remember:** This is YOUR training plan. Adjust rest days as needed to fit your life. Consistency beats perfection. Trust the process!
