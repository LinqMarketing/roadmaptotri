# Professional Triathlon Training Plans

Based on research of industry-leading training programs from Triathlete.com, Garmin, and professional coaching organizations, here are the recommended training structures for your app:

## Sprint Triathlon Programs

### 12-Week Sprint Triathlon (Beginner)
**Source:** Triathlete.com Super Simple Sprint Plan  
**Target:** Beginner to intermediate athletes  
**Weekly Structure:** 5-6 training days per week

**Program Overview:**
- 3 four-week cycles with progressive phases (Test, Build, Recover)
- Gradual volume increases with recovery weeks
- Weekly pattern: 1 swim test/build, 1 easy swim, 2-3 bike sessions, 2-3 run sessions
- Peak training: 5-6 hours per week

**Key Features:**
- Easy-to-follow rhythm with familiar workouts
- Measurable progress through repeated sessions
- Built-in recovery weeks every 4 weeks
- Transition work and brick workouts included

---

### 18-Week Sprint Triathlon (Intermediate)
**Extended version of 12-week plan**  
**Target:** Athletes with some triathlon experience  
**Weekly Structure:** 5-6 training days per week

**Program Overview:**
- 4 progressive phases with extended base building
- Increased volume and intensity compared to 12-week plan
- Includes speed work, tempo sessions, and race-pace efforts
- Peak training: 6-7 hours per week

**Key Features:**
- More advanced interval training
- Multiple brick workouts per week
- Open water swim preparation
- Race simulation sessions

---

### 24-Week Sprint Triathlon (Beginner-Friendly Extended)
**Extended beginner program**  
**Target:** Complete beginners or those returning from injury  
**Weekly Structure:** 4-5 training days per week

**Program Overview:**
- 6 progressive phases with extended base building period
- Slower progression with more recovery weeks
- Emphasis on technique development and aerobic base
- Peak training: 5-6 hours per week

**Key Features:**
- Gradual introduction to each discipline
- Extended technique focus
- More recovery days built in
- Lower injury risk

## Half Ironman (70.3) Programs

### 8-Week Half Ironman (Your Current Plan)
**Target:** Experienced triathletes with good base fitness  
**Weekly Structure:** 6 training days per week

**Program Overview:**
- Intensive 8-week peak phase
- Assumes solid aerobic base already established
- High volume weeks: 8-10 hours per week
- Includes long course simulation

**Key Features:**
- High-intensity intervals
- Long brick workouts
- Race-pace efforts
- Taper week included

---

### 24-Week Half Ironman (6-Month Beginner)
**Source:** Industry-standard beginner 70.3 program  
**Target:** Complete beginners to half ironman distance  
**Weekly Structure:** 4-5 training days per week initially, 5-6 at peak

**Program Overview:**
- 6 progressive phases over 24 weeks
- Phase 1-2 (Weeks 1-8): Base building with low volume
- Phase 3-4 (Weeks 9-16): Build phase with increasing volume
- Phase 5 (Weeks 17-22): Peak phase with race-specific work
- Phase 6 (Weeks 23-24): Taper and race week

**Weekly Progression:**
- Weeks 1-4: 4-5 hours per week
- Weeks 5-12: 5-6 hours per week
- Weeks 13-20: 7-9 hours per week
- Weeks 21-24: 6-8 hours per week (taper)

**Key Features:**
- Beginner-friendly progression
- Emphasis on aerobic base development
- Gradual volume increases (10% per week max)
- Built-in recovery weeks every 3-4 weeks
- Long course simulation in final 8 weeks
- Open water swim preparation
- Transition practice and brick workouts
- Race-specific nutrition practice

## Training Plan Structure Template

Each professional training plan includes:

1. **Weekly Schedule:** Day-by-day breakdown with workout types
2. **Workout Descriptions:**
   - WU (Warm-up): Easy effort to prepare body
   - MS (Main Set): Primary workout focus
   - CD (Cool-down): Easy effort to recover
   - TP (Test Pace): Individual pace determined by testing
   - RI (Recovery Interval): Easy effort between hard efforts

3. **Training Zones:**
   - Easy/Conversational: 60-70% max heart rate
   - Moderate/Steady: 70-80% max heart rate
   - Threshold/Tempo: 80-90% max heart rate
   - VO2 Max/Interval: 90-100% max heart rate

4. **Recovery Guidance:**
   - Nutrition recommendations post-workout
   - Sleep and rest day importance
   - Cross-training options
   - Injury prevention strategies

5. **Testing Protocols:**
   - How to determine your test pace
   - When to retest and adjust
   - Pacing guidelines for race day

## Integration Recommendations

For your app, consider:

1. **PDF Downloads:** Professional formatted PDFs with weekly calendars
2. **Interactive Calendar:** Users can sync with their calendar app
3. **Workout Descriptions:** In-app explanations of all training terminology
4. **Progress Tracking:** Link training logs to nutrition and supplement tracking
5. **Notifications:** Reminders for upcoming workouts
6. **Customization:** Allow users to adjust rest days based on their schedule
