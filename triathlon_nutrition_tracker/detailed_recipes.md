## NOTES FOR SUCCESS

**Meal Prep Strategy:**
- Prepare proteins (chicken, turkey, salmon, steak) in bulk on Sunday for the week
- Cook rice and quinoa in batches
- Pre-chop vegetables and store in airtight containers
- Store cooked meals in glass containers for up to 4 days

**Portion Control:**
- Use a food scale for accuracy with proteins and grains
- Measure oils carefully—they add up quickly in calories
- Pre-portion snacks into containers for grab-and-go convenience

**Hydration:**
- Drink 8oz water with each meal
- Add electrolytes to water during and after training sessions
- Aim for 3-4 liters of water daily

**Flexibility:**
- Swap proteins: chicken ↔ turkey ↔ fish (adjust portions for similar calories)
- Swap carbs: rice ↔ quinoa ↔ potatoes (similar portions work)
- Add vegetables freely—they're low calorie and nutrient-dense

**Budget Tips:**
- Buy proteins on sale and freeze
- Buy rice and oats in bulk
- Use frozen vegetables (same nutrition, cheaper than fresh)
- Make energy balls in batches and freeze
