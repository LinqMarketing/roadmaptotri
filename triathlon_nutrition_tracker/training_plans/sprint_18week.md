# 18-Week Sprint Triathlon Training Plan
## Intermediate Progression

**Race Distance:** 750m Swim | 20km Bike | 5km Run  
**Target Audience:** Athletes with some triathlon experience or returning from break  
**Weekly Training:** 5-6 days per week | 5-7 hours total  
**Program Structure:** 4.5 cycles with extended build and intensity phases

---

## Program Overview

This 18-week intermediate program extends the 12-week plan with longer build phases and more advanced interval work. It's designed for athletes who want more time to develop fitness or are returning to racing after a break.

**Key Principles:**
- Extended base building period
- Progressive intensity increases
- More advanced interval training
- Multiple brick workouts per week
- Open water swim preparation

---

## CYCLE 1: BASE BUILDING (Weeks 1-5)

### Week 1: Test Week

**Monday:** Rest day

**Tuesday:** Swim Test (30 min)
- Warm-up: 5-10 min easy swim
- Main Set: Swim 15 min max distance
- Cool-down: 5 min easy swim
- *Record distance for baseline*

**Wednesday:** Easy Bike (45 min)

**Thursday:** Run Test (45 min)
- Warm-up: 10 min easy walk/jog
- Main Set: Run/walk 30 min max distance
- Cool-down: 5 min easy walk

**Friday:** Easy Swim (20 min)

**Saturday:** Bike Test (45 min)
- Warm-up: 10 min easy
- Main Set: Bike 30 min max distance
- Cool-down: 5 min easy

**Sunday:** Easy Run (30 min)

**Weekly Volume:** ~4 hours

## Training Terminology

**Test Pace (TP):** The pace you established during your test weeks.

**Easy/Conversational Pace:** You should be able to hold a conversation.

**Warm-up (WU):** Gradually increase heart rate and blood flow.

**Main Set (MS):** The primary focus of the workout.

**Cool-down (CD):** Gradually decrease intensity to aid recovery.

**Recovery Interval (RI):** Easy effort between hard efforts.

---

## Nutrition During Training

**Pre-Workout (30-60 min before):**
- Banana with almond butter
- Toast with jam
- Rice cakes with honey

**During Workout (sessions >90 min):**
- Water with electrolytes
- Energy gels or sports drink

**Post-Workout (within 30 min):**
- Protein + carbs (chicken with rice, protein smoothie, turkey sandwich)
- Rehydrate with electrolytes

---

## Recovery & Injury Prevention

**Sleep:** Aim for 7-9 hours per night.

**Stretching:** 10-15 min after each workout.

**Foam Rolling:** 5-10 min on rest days.

**Listen to Your Body:** If something hurts, take an extra rest day.

---

## Race Week Checklist

- [ ] Lay out all gear the night before
- [ ] Check weather forecast
- [ ] Review race course map
- [ ] Plan parking and arrival time
- [ ] Practice race-day breakfast
- [ ] Get good sleep 2-3 nights before
- [ ] Reduce training volume by 70%
- [ ] Maintain intensity but reduce duration
- [ ] Eat familiar foods only
- [ ] Hydrate consistently

---

**Remember:** This is YOUR training plan. Adjust rest days as needed to fit your life. Consistency beats perfection. Trust the process!
