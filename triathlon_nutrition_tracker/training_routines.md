# Triathlon Training Routines

## 1. Sprint Triathlon: 12-Week Beginner Program

**Distance:** 750m swim | 20km bike | 5km run

### Overview
This 12-week program is designed for absolute beginners who want to complete a sprint triathlon. The focus is on building basic fitness across all three disciplines while maintaining balance and avoiding injury.

### Weekly Training Schedule

**Week 1-2: Foundation Building**
- **Monday:** Rest or easy yoga (20 min)
- **Tuesday:** Swim 15 min (easy pace, focus on form)
- **Wednesday:** Run 15 min (easy pace, walk/run intervals)
- **Thursday:** Rest
- **Friday:** Bike 20 min (easy pace, flat terrain)
- **Saturday:** Long run 20 min (easy pace)
- **Sunday:** Rest

**Week 3-4: Building Endurance**
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 20 min (focus on steady pace)
- **Wednesday:** Run 20 min (continuous, easy pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 25 min (flat terrain, steady pace)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long run 25 min
- **Sunday:** Rest

**Week 5-6: Increasing Volume**
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 25 min (steady pace)
- **Wednesday:** Run 25 min (include 2-3 x 2 min at moderate pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 30 min (include some rolling terrain)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long run 30 min
- **Sunday:** Swim 20 min (easy, recovery pace)

**Week 7-8: Building Strength**
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 30 min (include 4 x 100m with 30 sec rest)
- **Wednesday:** Run 30 min (include 4 x 2 min at moderate pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 35 min (include hill repeats)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long run 35 min
- **Sunday:** Bike 25 min (easy, recovery pace)

**Week 9-10: Peak Training**
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 35 min (include 6 x 100m with 20 sec rest)
- **Wednesday:** Run 35 min (include 5 x 2 min at moderate-hard pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 40 min (include hill repeats and tempo sections)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long run 40 min
- **Sunday:** Swim 25 min (easy, recovery pace)

**Week 11: Taper Week 1**
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 25 min (easy pace)
- **Wednesday:** Run 20 min (easy pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 25 min (easy pace)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Easy 20 min run
- **Sunday:** Rest

**Week 12: Race Week**
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 15 min (easy, focus on form)
- **Wednesday:** Run 10 min (easy pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 15 min (easy pace)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Rest or 10 min easy swim
- **Sunday:** RACE DAY

### Nutrition Guidelines
- Eat 2-3 hours before training
- Hydrate with 500ml water during training sessions over 30 minutes
- Post-workout: Carbs + Protein within 30 minutes
- Daily: 2,000-2,200 kcal with balanced macros

## 2. Sprint Triathlon: 18-Week Intermediate Program

**Distance:** 750m swim | 20km bike | 5km run

### Overview
This 18-week program builds on the 12-week foundation with more structured training, including speed work and brick workouts (bike-run combinations).

### Key Training Phases

**Phase 1: Base Building (Weeks 1-4)**
- Swim: 3x per week, 25-35 min each
- Bike: 2x per week, 30-40 min each
- Run: 3x per week, 20-35 min each
- Total weekly volume: 4-5 hours

**Phase 2: Build (Weeks 5-10)**
- Swim: 3x per week, 35-45 min each (include speed work)
- Bike: 2x per week, 40-50 min each (include tempo sections)
- Run: 3x per week, 30-40 min each (include tempo runs)
- Brick workouts: 1x per week (30 min bike + 15 min run)
- Total weekly volume: 5-6 hours

**Phase 3: Peak (Weeks 11-15)**
- Swim: 3x per week, 40-50 min each (include race-pace intervals)
- Bike: 2x per week, 45-60 min each
- Run: 3x per week, 35-45 min each
- Brick workouts: 1-2x per week
- Total weekly volume: 6-7 hours

**Phase 4: Taper (Weeks 16-17)**
- Reduce volume by 40-50%
- Maintain intensity with short, sharp intervals
- Focus on recovery

**Week 18: Race Week**
- Light training only
- Rest 2-3 days before race

### Sample Week (Peak Phase)
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 45 min (include 6 x 100m at race pace)
- **Wednesday:** Run 40 min (include 5 x 3 min at tempo pace)
- **Thursday:** Bike 50 min (include 3 x 5 min at tempo pace)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Brick workout - Bike 40 min + Run 15 min
- **Sunday:** Long run 45 min (easy pace)

## 3. Sprint Triathlon: 24-Week Beginner-Friendly Program

**Distance:** 750m swim | 20km bike | 5km run

### Overview
This 24-week program is ideal for complete beginners with limited fitness background. It allows for more gradual progression and includes more recovery weeks.

### Training Structure

**Weeks 1-4: Foundation**
- Swim: 2x per week, 15-20 min
- Bike: 2x per week, 20-30 min
- Run: 2x per week, 15-25 min
- Total: 2-3 hours per week

**Weeks 5-8: Building Base**
- Swim: 2-3x per week, 20-30 min
- Bike: 2x per week, 30-40 min
- Run: 2-3x per week, 20-30 min
- Total: 3-4 hours per week

**Weeks 9-12: Intermediate Build**
- Swim: 3x per week, 30-40 min
- Bike: 2-3x per week, 35-45 min
- Run: 3x per week, 25-35 min
- Total: 4-5 hours per week

**Weeks 13-16: Increasing Intensity**
- Swim: 3x per week, 35-45 min (include speed work)
- Bike: 3x per week, 40-50 min (include tempo)
- Run: 3x per week, 30-40 min (include tempo)
- Brick: 1x per week
- Total: 5-6 hours per week

**Weeks 17-20: Peak Building**
- Swim: 3x per week, 40-50 min
- Bike: 3x per week, 45-55 min
- Run: 3x per week, 35-45 min
- Brick: 1-2x per week
- Total: 6-7 hours per week

**Weeks 21-22: Recovery Week**
- Reduce volume by 40%
- Maintain intensity but shorter duration

**Weeks 23-24: Final Taper & Race**
- Week 23: Light training, 50% volume
- Week 24: Race week, minimal training

## 5. Half Ironman (70.3): 6-Month Beginner Program

**Distance:** 1.9km swim | 90km bike | 21.1km run

### Overview
This 24-week (6-month) program takes someone from beginner fitness to half ironman ready. It includes more gradual progression and recovery weeks.

### Training Phases

**Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)**
- **Focus:** Build aerobic base, establish routine
- **Swim:** 2x per week, 20-30 min
- **Bike:** 2x per week, 30-40 min
- **Run:** 2x per week, 20-30 min
- **Total:** 3-4 hours per week

**Phase 2: Base Building (Weeks 5-8)**
- **Focus:** Increase volume, add second long session
- **Swim:** 2-3x per week, 30-40 min
- **Bike:** 2-3x per week, 40-50 min
- **Run:** 2-3x per week, 25-35 min
- **Total:** 4-5 hours per week

**Phase 3: Intermediate Build (Weeks 9-12)**
- **Focus:** Introduce speed work, add brick workouts
- **Swim:** 3x per week, 35-45 min
- **Bike:** 3x per week, 45-60 min
- **Run:** 3x per week, 30-40 min
- **Brick:** 1x per week
- **Total:** 5-6 hours per week

**Phase 4: Building Intensity (Weeks 13-16)**
- **Focus:** Increase intensity, build race-specific fitness
- **Swim:** 3x per week, 40-50 min (include speed work)
- **Bike:** 3x per week, 55-75 min (include tempo)
- **Run:** 3x per week, 35-45 min (include tempo)
- **Brick:** 1-2x per week
- **Total:** 6-7 hours per week

**Phase 5: Peak Building (Weeks 17-20)**
- **Focus:** Reach peak fitness, race-simulation workouts
- **Swim:** 3x per week, 45-60 min
- **Bike:** 3x per week, 70-90 min
- **Run:** 3x per week, 40-50 min
- **Brick:** 1-2x per week
- **Total:** 7-8 hours per week

**Phase 6: Taper & Race (Weeks 21-24)**
- **Week 21-22:** Reduce volume by 40%, maintain intensity
- **Week 23:** Reduce volume by 60%, light training
- **Week 24:** Race week, minimal training

### Sample Week (Peak Phase)
- **Monday:** Rest
- **Tuesday:** Swim 50 min (include 8 x 150m at race pace)
- **Wednesday:** Run 45 min (include 6 x 3 min at tempo)
- **Thursday:** Bike 80 min (include 4 x 5 min at tempo)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Brick workout - Bike 70 min + Run 20 min
- **Sunday:** Long run 50 min (easy pace)

### Nutrition Guidelines
- Daily: 2,500 kcal with balanced macros
- During long sessions: 60-90g carbs per hour
- Post-workout: Carbs + Protein within 30 minutes
- Hydration: 3-4 liters daily + electrolytes

## Training Tips for All Programs

### Pacing Guidelines
- **Easy pace:** Should be able to hold a conversation
- **Tempo pace:** Comfortably hard, can speak in short sentences
- **Race pace:** Hard effort, can only speak a few words
- **Hard pace:** Maximum effort, cannot speak

### Recovery
- Sleep 7-9 hours per night
- Take at least 1-2 complete rest days per week
- Include easy/recovery sessions
- Listen to your body and adjust as needed

### Injury Prevention
- Warm up 5-10 minutes before every session
- Cool down 5-10 minutes after every session
- Stretch 2-3x per week
- Strengthen core 2-3x per week
- Gradually increase volume (no more than 10% per week)

### Nutrition During Training
- Eat 2-3 hours before training
- Hydrate with water or sports drink
- For sessions over 60 minutes: consume 30-60g carbs per hour
- Post-workout: Carbs + Protein within 30 minutes

### Mental Preparation
- Visualize race day success
- Practice transitions during training
- Familiarize yourself with the race course
- Build confidence through consistent training
