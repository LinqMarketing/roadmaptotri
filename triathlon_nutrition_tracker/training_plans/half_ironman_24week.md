# 24-Week Half Ironman (70.3) Training Plan
## Beginner to Half Ironman Ready

**Race Distance:** 1.9km Swim | 90km Bike | 21.1km Run  
**Target Audience:** Beginners or those with sprint triathlon experience  
**Weekly Training:** 4-6 days per week | 5-10 hours total  
**Program Structure:** 6 phases over 24 weeks

---

## Program Overview

This comprehensive 24-week program takes you from beginner fitness to half ironman ready. The plan uses periodization principles with progressive phases: Base Building → Aerobic Development → Build Phase → Peak Phase → Race Simulation → Taper & Race.

**Key Principles:**
- Gradual volume progression (10% increase per week max)
- Recovery weeks every 3-4 weeks
- Long course simulation in final 8 weeks
- Brick workouts to practice transitions
- Nutrition practice during long sessions

## PHASE 1: BASE BUILDING (Weeks 1-4)

### Week 1: Assessment Week

**Monday:** Rest day

**Tuesday:** Swim Assessment (30 min)
- Warm-up: 5-10 min easy swim
- Main Set: Swim 20 min continuous (take breaks if needed)
- Cool-down: 5 min easy swim
- *Record distance for baseline*

**Wednesday:** Easy Bike (45 min)
- Entire session easy/conversational pace
- High cadence (90+ RPM)

**Thursday:** Run Assessment (45 min)
- Warm-up: 10 min easy walk/jog
- Main Set: Run/walk 30 min continuous
- Cool-down: 5 min easy walk
- *Record distance for baseline*

**Friday:** Easy Swim (20 min)
- Swim easy, focus on form

**Saturday:** Bike Assessment (60 min)
- Warm-up: 10 min easy
- Main Set: Bike 45 min continuous
- Cool-down: 5 min easy
- *Record distance for baseline*

**Sunday:** Easy Run (30 min)

**Weekly Volume:** ~4.5 hours

## Race-Day Nutrition Strategy

**2-3 Hours Before Race:**
- Familiar breakfast: oatmeal with banana and honey, or toast with jam
- Hydrate with water and electrolytes

**30 Min Before Race:**
- Light snack: banana or energy bar
- Final hydration

**During Swim:**
- Focus on pacing and breathing
- No nutrition needed (too short)

**Bike (90 km, ~3-4 hours):**
- Start nutrition at 45 min mark
- Every 45 min: 1 energy bar or 2 energy gels
- Every 30 min: 500ml sports drink or water with electrolytes
- Aim for 200-300 calories per hour

**Run (21.1 km, ~2-3 hours):**
- Start nutrition at 15 min mark
- Every 20 min: 1 energy gel or sports drink
- Walk through aid stations if needed
- Focus on hydration - drink at every station

**Post-Race:**
- Within 30 min: Protein + carbs (chocolate milk, recovery drink, sandwich)
- Rehydrate with electrolytes
- Rest and celebrate!

## Training Terminology

**Test Pace:** The pace you can sustain for 5-10 minutes of hard effort.

**Race Pace:** The pace you plan to hold during the actual race.

**Easy/Conversational Pace:** You should be able to speak in full sentences.

**Warm-up (WU):** Gradually increase heart rate before main set.

**Main Set (MS):** The primary focus of the workout.

**Cool-down (CD):** Gradually decrease intensity to aid recovery.

**Recovery Interval (RI):** Easy effort between hard efforts.

---

## Recovery & Injury Prevention

**Sleep:** Aim for 7-9 hours per night. This is when adaptation happens.

**Stretching:** 10-15 min after each workout, especially legs and shoulders.

**Foam Rolling:** 5-10 min on rest days for muscle recovery.

**Massage:** Monthly professional massage or self-massage with foam roller.

**Nutrition:** Eat protein + carbs within 30 min of every workout.

**Listen to Your Body:** If something hurts (not just tired), take an extra rest day.

**Cross-Training:** Optional easy yoga or swimming on rest days.
